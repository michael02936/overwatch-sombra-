Welcome to Overwatch

Overwatch is a team-based multiplayer game created by Blizzard Entertainment. Players choose different heroes and work together to complete objectives and defeat the opposing team.

Every hero has their own abilities, personality, and play style. Teamwork is important because each player has a different role to help their team win.

Meet Sombra

Sombra is one of the heroes in Overwatch. She is known for being a hacker who uses technology and stealth to get around the battlefield. She can disappear, teleport, hack enemies, and use her abilities to give her team an advantage.

Sombra is a clever and sneaky character who likes to stay one step ahead of everyone else. Her hacking abilities make her different from many other heroes in the game.
